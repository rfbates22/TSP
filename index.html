User Guide & Instructions

1. Getting Started & Portfolio Setup

This tool helps federal employees and retirees estimate daily price fluctuations, asset allocation balances, and long-term multi-year projections for Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) Lifecycle (L) Funds.

2. Header Controls & Configuration Options

The top control panel features all key toggles and selector controls in order from left to right:

  • Select L Fund (Far Left): Choose your target Lifecycle Fund from the dropdown (e.g., L Income, L 2030, L 2060, L 2075) to update the dashboard's calculated metrics and charts.
  • Allocation Source: Toggle between Manual Allocations (custom user-edited mix) and Downloaded Allocations (preset benchmark values).
  • Market Hours Filter: When turned ON, daily returns freeze at 0.00% outside standard market trading hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM Eastern). Turn OFF to simulate movement at any time.
  • Real-Time API Stream: Turn ON to simulate continuous live market price stream ticks every 5 seconds.
  • Table View Mode: Toggle Compact View to reduce table padding and font size for dense data views or smaller screens.

3. Managing Asset Allocations & Risk Analysis

Click the Edit Allocations button to adjust the underlying G, F, C, S, and I Fund breakdown for your active L Fund. Total allocations must sum to exactly 100.00%.

  • Bonds vs Stocks Breakdown: Automatically aggregates G & F Funds into Bonds, and C, S, & I Funds into Stocks.
  • Risk Level Classification: Dynamic risk tiers based on total stock exposure:
    • Low: < 30% Stocks
    • Mod-Low: 30% – 49.9% Stocks
    • Moderate: 50% – 69.9% Stocks
    • High: 70% – 84.9% Stocks
    • Very High: 85%+ Stocks

4. Long-Term Return Predictions

Click Return Predictions to project growth over 1, 5, 10, and 20 years across all L Funds based on historical benchmark returns and a standard $10,000 portfolio baseline.
